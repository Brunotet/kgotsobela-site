Our story

Industrial & commercial HVAC specialists.

Kgotsobela Consortium is a mechanical engineering and HVAC service provider based in Mbombela, Mpumalanga. We specialise in the installation, maintenance, repair and servicing of industrial and commercial heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems.

"We strive for greatness."

We are committed to delivering reliable, efficient and cost-effective engineering solutions that ensure optimal performance, energy efficiency and indoor comfort for our clients — backed by a team that works with integrity and professionalism on every project.

Company history

Growing for a greater future.

From a small engineering service provider to a trusted HVAC and mechanical engineering partner for government, commercial and industrial clients across Mpumalanga.

2012

Founded & academic foundation

Kgotsobela Consortium was founded by Kgomotso Thibela, a passionate entrepreneur with a strong vision for excellence in engineering and technical services. The founder holds a National Diploma in Power Engineering, which laid the foundation for the technical expertise and discipline that drive our business.

2012 – 2014

Early growth

The company started as a small engineering service provider, offering general maintenance and technical solutions to commercial and industrial clients.

2014 – 2017

Expansion into HVAC

We expanded our expertise into the HVAC industry, specialising in heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems for industrial and commercial applications.

2017 – 2020

Building trust & capacity

Through consistent quality work and reliability, we grew our team, invested in advanced tools and equipment, and built long-term relationships with public and private sector clients.

2020 – 2024

Public & private sector experience

We gained valuable experience working with Government Departments, Municipalities, SANDF, and leading commercial clients, delivering large-scale HVAC and mechanical engineering projects.

2024 & Beyond

Our future

We remain committed to excellence, innovation and sustainable engineering solutions, while continuously growing our capacity to serve South Africa and beyond.
