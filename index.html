About the consortium

Engineering solutions you can rely on.

Kgotsobela Consortium is a mechanical engineering and HVAC service provider based in Mbombela. We specialise in the installation, maintenance, repair and servicing of industrial and commercial heating, ventilation, air conditioning and refrigeration systems — for clients who can't afford downtime.

"We strive for greatness."

Founded in 2012 by Kgomotso Thibela, a Power Engineering graduate, the company has grown from general maintenance work into a trusted HVAC and mechanical engineering partner for government, commercial and industrial clients across Mpumalanga.
